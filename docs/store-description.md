## Full Description

<h2>Outsmart the Dragon. Survive the Tournament.</h2>

Dragon Tactics is a turn-based card and board game where a party of up to four heroes battles a dragon boss on a compact 3×5 grid. Every move is telegraphed, every card has a purpose, and the dragon is always one step ahead — unless you're smarter.

<h3>Core Gameplay</h3>
Each round, dice determine action order. On your turn, choose to play one card from your hand — move, attack, heal, scout, or lay a trap — or discard everything to reassign your secret mission. The dragon's next attacks are revealed in advance: positioning and timing are everything.

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>4 Playable Races</b> — Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, each with unique passive abilities</li>
  <li><b>40-card shared deck</b> — movement, attack, stealth, healing, scouting, and rare treasures</li>
  <li><b>Hidden Missions</b> — one required, one optional; some cooperative, some adversarial</li>
  <li><b>Dragon AI with telegraphed attacks</b> — read the boss's next move and plan accordingly</li>
  <li><b>3-Match Tournament</b> — cumulative scoring across three matches decides the winner</li>
  <li><b>AI party members</b> — fight alongside 2–4 rule-based allies</li>
</ul>

<h3>Design Philosophy</h3>
Dragon Tactics delivers strategic depth in short bursts. Each match runs 10–20 minutes. No account required, no internet connection needed, no filler — just tactics.
