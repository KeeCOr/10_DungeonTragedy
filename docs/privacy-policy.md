# Privacy Policy — Dragon Tactics

**Last updated: 2026-08-30**

## Overview

Dragon Tactics is a browser-based game. This policy describes what data is (and is not) collected.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

Dragon Tactics does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. Specifically:

- No account registration or login required
- No names, email addresses, or contact information collected
- No gameplay telemetry or analytics sent to any server
- No crash reports uploaded automatically
- No cookies set beyond what is strictly necessary to run the game in the browser
- No advertising identifiers used

## Local Session Data

Any game state is stored **locally in your browser's memory or localStorage only**. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

## Third-Party Services

Dragon Tactics does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social platforms.

## Children

Dragon Tactics does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
